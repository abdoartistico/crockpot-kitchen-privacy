Privacy Policy

Crockpot Kitchen · Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how the Crockpot Kitchen publishing tool ("the app") handles information. The app is a private tool used only by the owner of the Crockpot Kitchen Facebook Page to upload and schedule recipe videos to that Page.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store, or process personal information about visitors, followers, or any other Facebook users. It does not read comments, messages, or profiles, and it does not use cookies or tracking.

The only data the app handles is the Page owner's own content (recipe videos and their titles) and the access token needed to publish that content to the owner's own Page.

How information is used

The access token is used solely to upload and schedule videos on the Crockpot Kitchen Page. It is stored privately on the owner's computer and is never shared, sold, or transferred to anyone.

Data sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. Content is sent only to Facebook (Meta Platforms) for publishing on the owner's Page, under Meta's own terms and policies.

Data deletion

The app keeps no user data, so there is nothing to delete on our side. If you have any request regarding your data, contact us through our Facebook Page and we will respond promptly. You can also remove the app's access at any time in your Facebook settings under Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
